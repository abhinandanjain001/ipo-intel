GO BEYOND THE PREMIUM

Your IPO research checklist

Business and financials

Read the prospectus for revenue, profitability, cash flow, debt and customer concentration. Compare trends across several reporting periods.

Valuation and use of proceeds

Compare valuation with relevant listed peers. Review how much of the issue raises fresh capital and how much is an offer for sale, then examine the stated use of funds.

Demand and allotment

Review category-wise subscriptions from official exchange data. Subscription multiples describe demand; they do not provide a reliable allotment probability or guarantee returns.

Liquidity and exit planning

Check applicable lot and trading rules, especially for SME issues. Plan your holding period and the loss you can absorb before committing money.

The feed covers offer data and unofficial GMP. Company fundamentals, analyst ratings and current stock prices are not supplied here.

How the estimate works

The base listing estimate is issue price plus reported GMP. Bearish and bullish prices subtract or add your scenario spread, measured against issue price. Without GMP, the base uses the issue price.

Profit/loss = (listing price − issue price) × allotted shares − costs. The spread is an assumption, not a statistical confidence interval. Allotment is not guaranteed. These scenarios are not backtested predictions.

Know what the data means

The web feed refreshes every five minutes while this page is open. Fetch time and the source's reading time are different; check both. Unknown readings stay unknown.

GMP is unofficial and can change before listing. Fees and taxes depend on your circumstances; enter your estimated total. Review the prospectus and official exchange notices before investing.
