How the estimate works

The base listing estimate is issue price plus reported GMP. Bearish and bullish prices subtract or add your scenario spread, measured against issue price. Without GMP, the base uses the issue price.

Profit/loss = (listing price − issue price) × allotted shares − costs. The spread is an assumption, not a statistical confidence interval. Allotment is not guaranteed. These scenarios are not backtested predictions.

Know what the data means

The web feed refreshes every five minutes while this page is open. Fetch time and the source's reading time are different; check both. Unknown readings stay unknown.

GMP is unofficial and can change before listing. Fees and taxes depend on your circumstances; enter your estimated total. Review the prospectus and official exchange notices before investing.
